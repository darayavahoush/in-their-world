# About In Their World

Reading a checklist about autism, ADHD, dyslexia, or a speech/language
difference tells you the facts — it doesn't tell you what a child's
morning actually feels like. **In Their World** is four short simulations,
one per condition, that put a parent or teacher inside that experience for
a few minutes: sounds you can't tune out, focus that keeps slipping,
letters that won't sit still, words that won't come out in time.

Most parents and teachers don't get long before they need to understand —
this is meant to be a five-minute way in, not a diagnostic tool.

## The four modules

- **Autism** — a sensory-overload task: find the real target while sound
  cues and visual noise compete for attention, the way an overstimulating
  room can.
- **ADHD** — a focus task with distractions popping in constantly, showing
  how much effort it takes just to stay on one thing.
- **Dyslexia** — a reading passage with letters that jitter and swim,
  followed by a short comprehension check under that condition.
- **Speech/language** — a push-through-the-block simulation of a word that
  won't come out while a conversation partner waits.

Each module follows the same three-part shape:

1. **Feel it first** — the simulation itself, not a description of it.
2. **See the numbers** — prevalence data and context, so the scale feels
   real rather than abstract.
3. **Know what helps** — concrete, low-effort strategies for home and
   classroom, not just awareness.

## Why the data is Indian

Most awareness material in this space leans on US or UK prevalence
numbers, which don't map cleanly onto a country where diagnosis is far
less common and often comes late. The figures in this app are drawn from
the Dyslexia Association of India, AIISH, and INCLEN Trust / PLOS Medicine
studies instead, so the scale feels like *here*, not somewhere else.

## Who it's for

Parents and teachers who want a starting point, not a stereotype —
five minutes of friction, not a lecture.
